Week 4 – Evaluation Report

In Week 4 I evaluated my virtual try-on model. 
I compared the baseline model and the improved model using two metrics SSIM and LPIPS. 
SSIM shows structural similarity, and LPIPS shows human-perceived visual difference.

The improved model performed better. 
It produced a more realistic try-on result due to segmentation and augmentation. 
The graphs and metrics table confirm that the improved version is higher quality than the baseline.

This completes the evaluation stage of the project.
