Week 3 – Improved Model Report

In Week 3 I improved my virtual try-on model. 
First, I added segmentation using rembg so the system can separate the person from the background. 
Then, I added data augmentation for the clothing brightness change, contrast change, and rotation. 
These improvements helped the clothes look more natural on the person.

I also compared the baseline model and the improved model. 
The improved version gives a more realistic result and fits the clothing better.

Metrics
- SSIM shows the structural difference between baseline and improved.
- LPIPS shows the visual difference based on human perception.

Overall, the improved model works better and produces more realistic virtual try-on images.
